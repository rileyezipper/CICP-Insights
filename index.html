About the Analysis

These reports provide data-driven insights into Indiana's advanced industries ecosystem, examining employment trends, wage dynamics, regional concentrations, and economic productivity. The analysis covers seven key economic development initiatives:

  • Advanced & Traded Industries: Manufacturing and traded sectors with high productivity
  • AgriNovus: Agriculture and food production innovation
  • BioCrossroads: Life sciences and biotechnology
  • Conexus: Energy, logistics, and advanced manufacturing
  • TechPoint: Technology and digital innovation
  • Finance & Insurance: Financial services
  • Healthcare: Health services and related industries

Data sources include the Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW) via Lightcast, Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA), Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), and BEA R&D Satellite Account.
